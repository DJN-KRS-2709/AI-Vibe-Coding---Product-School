Scenario Brief
Paste this as your prompt's Context block
Company
B2B project management SaaS. 5,000 paying teams. $4.2M ARR. Series A. 18 months old.
Problem
30% of new customers churn within 90 days. The company cannot raise Series B with this churn rate. The board has given the team one quarter to show measurable improvement.
Key Data
Users who invite a teammate in the first 3 days retain at 3x the rate (68% vs 22%). Only 12% of new users send an invite in the first 3 days. The invite button is buried in Settings > Team > Members. 60% of new users never create their first task.
Constraints
No engineering resources for 6 weeks. Must work within the existing product. Prototype only.
Your Job
Prototype the intervention with the highest likelihood of reducing 90-day churn. Identify the riskiest assumption, then build to test it.
Competing Hypotheses
  • VP Customer Success
    "It's a team activation problem. Make inviting effortless."
  • Head of Product
    "It's a feature discovery problem. Users don't find the features that make us sticky."
  • CEO
    "I don't care what it is. Cut 90-day churn in half."

Company
Mid-market SaaS. 200-person sales team across 3 offices + remote. $45M ARR. Series C.
Problem

Prototype an intervention that gets adoption above 50% without requiring training. Identify the real blocker, then build to test.

Problem

Scenario Brief
Paste this as your prompt's Context block
Company
Customer-facing analytics platform. 14,200 MAU. $8.5M ARR. Series B. 3 years old.
Problem
60% first-visit bounce rate. Dashboard built for data analysts but user base is now 80% non-technical business users. New users see 12 charts and leave within 30 seconds. Power users (20%) love it — NPS of 72.
Key Data
12 charts on default view with no hierarchy. Only 8% discover custom views (76% of power users use them). 54% rely on weekly email summary. 19% export to Google Sheets. 38% of support tickets (~412/mo) are dashboard comprehension questions.
Constraints
Cannot remove charts from power user view. Must serve both audiences. Data pipeline is solid — problem is presentation. Prototype only.
Your Job
Prototype the intervention that gets first-visit bounce below 30% for non-technical users while preserving the power user experience.
Competing Hypotheses
  • PM Lead
    "Information overload. We need a simplified default view for non-technical users."
  • Designer
    "It's a hierarchy problem. Nothing tells users where to look first."
  • Engineering
    "Just add a tutorial. Problem solved."
  • Customer Success
    "We're drowning in tickets. Whatever you build, make it self-explanatory."
