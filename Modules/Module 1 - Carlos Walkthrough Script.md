## Slide 17 — Tool vs. Toy

**On screen:**  
Tool — Did it help you decide something? Did you learn if your assumption was right or wrong? Did it move you from uncertainty to clarity? Tools generate decisions. They answer questions. They reduce risk.  
Toy — It looked cool but taught you nothing. It impressed people in a demo but didn't validate any hypothesis. Toys generate applause, not insights.  
The entire course is about building tools, not toys. Speed without learning is just motion. The goal isn't to build fast—it's to learn fast.

**Say:**
