The Rules

1
15 minutes. Work solo. No collaboration. This is about your instincts and speed.
2
No methodology. No framework. Just open Lovable and go. Build on instinct.
3
Aim for clickable and showable. Not perfect. Not polished. Just something you can share via link.
4
Stuck? Prompt again. Still stuck? Prompt differently. Keep moving.
🧊
Hit blank-page syndrome? Two escape routes below.

Escape Route 1: Copy and Customize

Starter Prompts — Replace the [brackets] and go
"Build me a [community/social] feature for [fitness/sales/customer] users. Include a [matching screen / leaderboard / shared feed]."

"Build me a dashboard that shows [account health / sales pipeline / user activity]. Include a [filter / list / chart]."

"Build me a [type of app] that helps users [one action]. Include [one UI element]."

Escape Route 2: First Screen Method

Ask yourself → Then prompt
What's the first screen your user would see? What's on it?

→ "Build me a [that screen] for [that user] with [those key elements]."

Example: "Build me a sales rep dashboard with recent calls, CRM sync button, and quick-note field."

Show and Swap Your Prototype

Breakout · 10 minutes

Pair up with a peer and experience their prototype as a real user.
This mirrors actual user behavior and gives you raw feedback on whether your vibe translates into a functional tool.

1
Swap prototype links in the chat
30 seconds
Drop your shareable URL. Grab your partner's. No verbal context — no setup, no apologies, no "what I was going for was…". Let the build speak for itself.
2
Silent walk through your partner's build
3 minutes
Click as if you've never seen it. Don't narrate. Take notes on the three questions below — you'll discuss them in step 3.
3
Discuss — then swap roles and repeat
6 minutes (3 + 3)
Walk through the three questions with your partner. Be specific — "the third button confused me because it looked clickable but wasn't" beats "it's a bit confusing." Then swap roles and repeat the whole loop on your build.

The three discussion questions

1
What you understood immediately about the product's purpose.
2
What confused you about the prototype.
3
What assumption you thought this prototype was testing.
🤐
The silent rule is the whole point. Three minutes of no commentary is hard. The comment you most want to make at minute 2 — write it down instead. That note is the most useful signal of the whole exercise.
🧑‍💻
No partner available? Fall back to a silent self-walk + the AI-review prompt below. Open your link in a fresh tab, click as a first-time user, then paste the prompt + your URL into ChatGPT or Claude.
AI-Review Prompt — solo fallback (copy verbatim)
You are a senior product reviewer. I built a prototype to explore the following assumption: [paste your one-sentence assumption]. The link is: [paste]. Critique the build on three dimensions: (1) Does the first screen communicate the core value in under 10 seconds? (2) What's the single biggest source of confusion? (3) What's the one change that would most increase my confidence in this direction? End with the question you would ask the user if you could only ask one.
