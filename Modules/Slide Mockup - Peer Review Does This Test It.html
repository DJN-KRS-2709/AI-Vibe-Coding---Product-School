If your partner built a dashboard instead of a product, call it out. That's the most useful feedback you can give.

Speaker Notes

Setup (30 sec): "Breakout rooms. Groups of 2. You have 10 minutes total. First 3 minutes: silence. Open your partner's Lovable link. Click through it the way a real user would — no context, no explanation. Can you figure out what it does? Can you figure out what it's testing?"

On the silent phase: "The silence is the test. If your partner needs to explain what their prototype does, the prototype doesn't work. A real user won't have your partner standing behind them narrating. The prototype needs to speak for itself."

On the four questions: "After the silent phase, discuss. Four questions on screen. The most important one is number 4: Tool vs. Toy. Can the builder actually use this prototype to make a go/no-go decision on their hypothesis? Or does it just look impressive? An impressive dashboard that doesn't test anything is worse than an ugly prototype that answers the question."

On real data: "Question 3 — can you see real numbers and real quotes from the scenario? This is the Module 2 difference. In Module 1, everyone used placeholder text. 'Lorem ipsum' doesn't convince anyone. Real data makes the prototype credible. If your partner's prototype says 'XX% churn rate' instead of '30% churn rate,' that's a miss."

Transition: "When you come back, I'm going to ask: how many of you built a tool that tests something, and how many of you built a toy that looks good? Be honest. That distinction is the entire point of this module."
