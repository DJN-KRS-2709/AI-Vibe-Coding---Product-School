## Slide 10 — Breakout Group Activity: Show and Swap
**Breakout Group Activity: Show and Swap** | 10 Minutes

**01** Person A shares link. Person B clicks through as a user. Person A watches silently—no explaining allowed.

**02** Feedback (2 minutes): What I understood / What confused me / What assumption does this test?

**03** Switch roles. Person B shares, Person A uses. Repeat the feedback cycle.

**The rule:** You are NOT allowed to explain your prototype. Just share the link and watch them use it. This mirrors real user behavior—they won't have you there to explain.

**Format:** Breakout rooms. Instructor: "How many per room? Press the button. Don't suffer." (Carlos: Random assignment fine—final project is individual.)

## Slide 17 — Tool vs. Toy
**Tool vs. Toy**

**Tool** — Did it help you decide something? Did you learn if your assumption was right or wrong? Did it move you from uncertainty to clarity? Tools generate decisions. They answer questions. They reduce risk.

**Toy** — It looked cool but taught you nothing. It impressed people in a demo but didn't validate or invalidate any hypothesis. Toys generate applause, not insights.

The entire Vibe Coding course is about building tools, not toys. Speed without learning is just motion. The goal isn't to build fast—it's to learn fast.
