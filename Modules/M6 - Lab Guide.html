Connect Analytics

Hands-On · 15 minutes

Your product has been live since M5. Let's see what's happening.

1
Review Lovable Insights
5 minutes
Open your deployed product in Lovable. Find the insights/analytics panel. Review what's already being tracked: page views, user sessions, feature usage. Screenshot your current metrics — this is your baseline.
2
Set Up Google Analytics (GA4)
5 minutes
a. Go to analytics.google.com
b. Create a new property (free)
c. Choose "Web" as the platform
d. Get your Measurement ID (starts with G-)
e. Use the prompt below to add it to your project
Analytics Setup Prompt
"Add Google Analytics tracking to this project. Use Measurement ID: G-XXXXXXXXXX. Add the gtag script to the head of the main HTML file. Track page views automatically."
⚠️
Replace G-XXXXXXXXXX with your actual Measurement ID from the GA4 setup. After adding the tag, redeploy your project.
3
Verify Tracking
5 minutes
Open your deployed product in a new tab. Go to GA4 → Real-time view. You should see yourself as a live user. Open on your phone — now you should see two live users.
Lovable insights reviewed — baseline screenshot taken
GA4 property created — Measurement ID obtained
GA4 tag added — prompt executed, redeployed
Tracking verified — see yourself in real-time view
💡
If GA4 setup takes too long: Lovable's built-in insights are enough for the AI analysis sprint. Don't let GA4 setup eat into analysis time.
2

AI Analysis Sprint

Hands-On · 15 minutes

Pull your data. Ask AI to evaluate whether your product is solving the problem you defined.

1
Gather Your Metrics
3 minutes
Open Lovable insights or GA4. Screenshot or copy your key metrics: sessions, user counts, feature usage, any engagement data you can find.
2
Find Your Problem Statement
2 minutes
Open 04-structure/PRD.md in your GitHub repo — that's where your Living PRD lives (source of truth across modules, not the M4 tool's browser cache). Copy your problem statement and your M2 hypothesis. Can't find them? Pull from 02-validation/validation-brief.md in the same repo, or write a one-sentence version from memory.
3
Run the AI Analysis
5 minutes
Open ChatGPT or Gemini. Use the prompt below. Paste your problem statement, hypothesis, and metrics.
AI Analysis Prompt
"I built a product to solve this problem: [paste your problem statement].

My hypothesis was: [paste your M2 hypothesis].

Here are the metrics from my deployed product: [paste your metrics data].

Evaluate: is this product solving the problem? What does the data suggest? What are the top 3 things I should improve, ranked by likely impact?"
4
Identify Your Top Finding
5 minutes
Read the AI's analysis. Highlight the single highest-impact recommendation. That's what you'll build next.
Metrics gathered — from Lovable insights or GA4
Problem statement + hypothesis — copied from 04-structure/PRD.md or 02-validation/validation-brief.md in your GitHub repo
AI analysis complete — evaluation received
Top finding identified — single highest-impact recommendation
🔥
The wow moment: When AI connects your data to your problem statement and tells you something you didn't expect about your own product. That analysis would take a product team a day. You just did it in 2 minutes.
3

Build One Improvement

Hands-On · 10 minutes

Pick your single highest-impact finding from the AI analysis. Build it. Redeploy.

1
Write Your Iteration Prompt
2 minutes
Use the pattern below. Reference the specific finding from your AI analysis.
Iteration Prompt Pattern
"Based on user data showing [specific finding from AI analysis], update [specific part of the product] to [specific change]. Keep all existing functionality working."
💡
Evidence-based vs. guess-based: Your prompt should reference the data that drove the decision. Not "I think the onboarding is confusing" but "Users drop off at step 3 — the CTA is unclear. Rewrite step 3."
2
Build the Improvement
5 minutes
Paste your iteration prompt into Lovable. Review the changes. Test that existing functionality still works.
3
Redeploy
3 minutes
Redeploy your project. Your live URL now reflects a data-driven improvement. Open it. Verify the change is live.
Iteration prompt written — references specific data finding
Improvement built — tested in Lovable
Redeployed — live URL reflects the change

You just completed the full product lifecycle:
understand → hypothesize → build → ship → measure → learn → improve

Quick Polish

Hands-On · 5 minutes

Quick cleanup before the gallery walk. One prompt per fix.

⏱️
5 minutes only. Don't start new features. Just clean up what's there.
Page title — shows your product name, not "Vite App"
Favicon — custom icon in the browser tab
Mobile responsive — check on your phone
No placeholder text — no "Lorem ipsum" or "TODO"
Live URL works in incognito
Polish Prompt — Page Title + Favicon
"Update the page title to '[Your Product Name]' and add a favicon that matches the product's brand. The favicon should be a simple, recognizable icon."
Polish Prompt — Mobile Responsive
"Make this app fully responsive for mobile screens. The navigation should collapse into a hamburger menu on screens narrower than 768px. All content should be readable without horizontal scrolling."
✅
Redeploy after fixes. Your live URL updates instantly. One final redeploy when time is up.

Gallery Walk

Experience Each Other's Products · 20 minutes

These products have been measured and improved with real data. You're using them as a real user.

1
Post your live URL in Slack
Make sure your latest deploy is live. Drop the URL in the class Slack channel.
2
Open 4–5 classmates' products
Pick products from different scenarios if possible. Open each in its own browser tab.
3
Real user test for each product
a. Sign up with a real email
b. Try the core flow — what is this product trying to do?
c. Try to break it — what happens?
d. Note: one thing that impressed you, one thing that confused you
4
Post feedback in Slack
Tag the builder. Keep it honest and specific.
Feedback Template
@[builder name] — used your product!
"One thing that impressed me: ___"
"One thing that confused me: ___"

Your 5-Minute Pitch

Presentations · 5 minutes each

No slides required. Your product IS the presentation.

1
The Problem
30 seconds
What user problem are you solving? One sentence.
2
The Hypothesis
30 seconds
What did you test? What was your M2 assumption?
3
Live Demo
2 minutes
Show your deployed product. Walk through the core flow. Show one key integration.
4
What the Data Told You
1 minute
What did analytics reveal? What did AI analysis surface? What improvement did you make based on evidence?
5
The Journey
1 minute
Show your M1 build vs. your M6 build. Side by side. What changed?
🔥
The M1 vs M6 side-by-side is the most powerful moment. Open both tabs before you present so you can switch quickly.
Live URL ready — tested and working
M1 URL ready — for the side-by-side comparison
Data story prepared — what analytics told you, what you improved
📹
Not presenting live? Record a 2-minute Loom walkthrough. Include your data story: what you measured, what you learned, what you improved. Post in Slack with your M1 link.
