Your Vibe Coding Tool Stack

Why Lovable?

Lovable's visual-first interface offers the lowest barrier for non-technical PMs. You don't need to understand code to build functional prototypes — and it's our main tool throughout the course.
💡 Important
Through our partnership with Product School, you get Pro access for free throughout the course. Your Pro package includes ~100 credits per month — not unlimited.
Credit-Saving Tip
Do your prompt planning in Gemini or ChatGPT. Paste the polished prompt into Lovable only when it's time to build. You're welcome to explore other tools in our AI Tools Perks on your own.
Skill Markdowns

Your New Superpower for Speed

AI tools like Cursor, Claude, and Lovable can import skills — pre-made instruction sets in markdown that give the AI specialized capabilities.
What's a Skill?
A text file that tells the AI: "Here's how to build dashboards for enterprise SaaS" or "Here's our design system: always use these colors." The AI follows it on every prompt.
Where to Find Them
Marketplaces and communities share skills for free — you don't need to create them. Download what others have built, like installing an app.
Why It Matters
By Module 3, you'll build your own skills inside your Living Prompt Pack. For now, know they exist. A superpower you can acquire without writing code.
Bridging Design and Build

Three Ways to Bridge Design

Modern Vibe Coding tools are multimodal — they ingest your existing assets and match your design system instantly.
1 · Text-to-App
Natural language prompts
Best for: new concepts where no design exists.
Strategic choice: 0-to-1 products.
2 · Screenshot-to-App
Upload a screenshot
Best for: cloning existing product patterns or ideas.
Strategic choice: 1-to-N products.
3 · Design-to-Code
Figma imports / design system docs
Best for: on-brand prototypes from existing systems (also code-to-design).
Strategic choice: 1-to-N products.

You're not ignoring your design system — you're ingesting it.

Vibe Coding Credit Efficiency

Spend Credits on Architecture, Not Trial-and-Error

Your credits represent your total capacity for experimentation. Strategic resource management ensures they're spent on high-value architectural shifts.
1
Plan in a Sandbox
Structure prompts and iterate logic in an external LLM. Do ~60% of refinement here before burning a single credit.
2
Paste into Tool
Move to Lovable only when the prompt is polished. Aim for one generation, not trial-and-error iterations on half-baked instructions.
3
Adjust Code Directly
Fix typos, colors, alignment in code. Save credits for major structural changes or new feature injections.
⚠️ The Eject Rule
If a prompt fails twice, stop. Export the code to an external tool to debug, then paste the fix back. Never enter a "credit death spiral" by re-prompting the same broken logic.

Instructor-Led Demo · 15 Minutes

Moving From Basic to Pro — The Art of Iteration

Demo 1 answered "What should I build?" Demo 2 answers "How do I make this real?"
The Problem
You have three working prototypes — but they all look like "vibe" sketches. Your VP of Design won't look at them because they don't match the brand. Your Engineering Lead says they're too generic to be useful.
Speed got you the idea. Now strategic iteration must get you the buy-in.
5 min · Step 1
Design System Matching
Feed a Mobbin screenshot into Lovable; redesign the prototype to match aesthetics, layout, and palette.
5 min · Step 2
Add Interactivity
Functional progress tracker, actionable recommendation cards, loading skeleton — clickable, not just pretty.
5 min · Step 3
Surgical Refinement
Use focused prompts to fix one weak section without rebuilding. Compounding improvements > starting over.
