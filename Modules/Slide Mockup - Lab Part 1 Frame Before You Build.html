Speaker Notes

Setup (1 min): "You have 12 minutes and three tasks. Do NOT open Lovable yet — the quality of your prototype depends entirely on what you prepare now. Step 1: open the Validation Brief, select your scenario — it auto-fills your hypothesis, risk type, kill switch, and fidelity level. Review it. Adjust anything that doesn't feel right. Step 2: open the Context Data Pack for your scenario. Highlight the quotes and metrics that matter, or hit 'Copy Everything' for the full package. Step 3: find a visual reference — browse Mobbin or screenshot your own product."

On the Validation Brief: "The auto-fill gives you a starting point, not the answer. Read the hypothesis it generates. Does it actually test the riskiest assumption? If not, rewrite it. Same for the kill switch — what's the one thing that, if wrong, means this whole prototype was a waste? Be honest about that."

On the Context Data Pack: "You have two options. You can curate: highlight 2-3 quotes and 3-5 metrics that specifically support your hypothesis. Or you can hit 'Copy Everything' and give the AI the full picture. Either works — curating is sharper, full context is faster."

On Visual Reference: "Don't skip this. The difference between a generic-looking prototype and one that looks like a real product is a single screenshot. Mobbin is your best friend. And you can upload multiple screenshots now — grab the layout from one, the style from another."

Release: "Go. 12 minutes. When you're done, post in Slack: your scenario, your hypothesis, your screenshot, and your highlighted quotes/metrics. If it's not in Slack, it doesn't count. All three of these feed directly into the Structured Prompt Builder in Part 2."
