Step 2 — Diverge, Don't Decorate

Speaker Notes

Setup (1 min): "You have 10 minutes. Two tasks. First: fill out the Problem Frame template for your chosen scenario. All six elements. Paste it in Slack when you're done. Second: before you open Lovable, write down 2-3 fundamentally different ways you could solve this problem. Not color variations — different approaches."

On Divergence: "If your three options are the same dashboard with different layouts, you haven't diverged. Push for structurally different solutions — a self-service tool vs. a guided walkthrough vs. an alert system. The goal is to see which shape of product actually tests your riskiest assumption."

On Fidelity Matching: "Look at the bottom of the slide. Match your build depth to your risk. If your riskiest assumption is value — 'will anyone even want this?' — don't build a full app. A landing page answers that faster. If your risk is feasibility — 'can the logic actually work?' — then yes, you need a functional prototype. Don't over-build."

Release: "Go. 10 minutes. Problem Frame first, then diverge. If you finish early, start drafting your first prompt — but don't paste it into Lovable yet. That's Part 2."
