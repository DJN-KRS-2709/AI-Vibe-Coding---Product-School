Speaker Notes

Step 1 — Identify the Hypothesis: Start with a clear "if/then" statement. If you provide the AI with a specific set of inputs, then the user will achieve a specific outcome. This prevents aimless prompting and ensures every version of your prototype has a job to do.

Step 2 — Define the Constraints: Before you build, define the boundaries — intent, logic, interaction patterns. These constraints ensure the AI stays on track and produces a tool that tests your specific assumption rather than a generic demo.

Step 3 — Build and Stress-Test: Your goal isn't just to see it work — it's to see where it breaks. Stress-test the outputs and the user flow to identify technical and usability gaps that would stay hidden in a document.

Step 4 — Synthesize and Pivot: Use the findings to refine your problem framing. Don't just move on — update your hypothesis based on evidence. This is how you iterate on strategy, not just on pixels.
