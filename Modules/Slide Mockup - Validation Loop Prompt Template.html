Speaker Notes

How to use this: "This is your pre-flight checklist. Before you open Lovable, fill in steps 1 and 2. That gives you the raw material for step 3 — your actual prompt. After you build and stress-test, step 4 captures what you learned. Then you loop. Each revolution through this template produces a better prototype and a sharper hypothesis."

On the structure: "Notice steps 1 and 2 are framing work — you do this before you touch any tool. Step 3 is the actual prompt you paste into Lovable. Step 4 is what you do after. The template maps the loop to a concrete workflow: frame, build, learn, repeat."

On the if/then/because: "The hypothesis isn't 'I want to build a dashboard.' It's 'If I surface team invite prompts during onboarding, then day-3 invite rates increase, because solo users don't know the feature exists.' That's testable. That's what drives the build."

Connect to labs: "In Lab Part 1, you'll fill in steps 1–2. In Lab Part 2, you'll assemble step 3 and build. In the peer review, your partner evaluates whether the prototype actually tests your step-1 hypothesis. Step 4 happens after feedback. This template is the thread that runs through everything today."
