Speaker Notes

Setup: "This is your commitment moment. The scenario you pick now is the one you carry through the rest of the course. Every lab, every prototype, every deliverable builds on this same problem. No switching after today."

Scenario 1 — Retention Engine: "You're a PM at a B2B SaaS company. 30% of users churn within 90 days. Leadership wants answers. Your job: build a prototype that helps you understand why they're leaving and test whether an intervention works. If your instinct is 'do users even want this solution?' — that's a value risk."

Scenario 2 — Internal Tool: "Your company built a CRM. 18% adoption. Nobody uses it. Why? Your job: figure out why adoption is so low and prototype a version that fits into how people actually work. This is a classic 1-to-N problem — the product exists, the users don't come."

Scenario 3 — Marketplace Trust: "You run a services marketplace. Bookings are flat because new providers have zero reviews. Buyers don't trust them. Your job: prototype a trust mechanism that gets first-time providers booked. Could be value risk (does trust even matter?) or feasibility (can you generate credible trust signals?)."

Scenario 4 — Dashboard: "Your analytics dashboard has a 60% bounce rate. The data is there but nobody finds insight. Your job: prototype a version that surfaces the right information so people actually use it. Likely a usability problem — the data exists, the interface doesn't serve it."

Option 5 — Bring Your Own: "If you have a real problem from your company, you can use that. Three requirements: a clear user, a measurable outcome, and enough ambiguity that you don't already know the answer. Check with me during the break if you're unsure."

Timing: "Don't overthink it. Pick the one that feels closest to your real work. You have until the end of break to decide. Post your choice in Slack."
