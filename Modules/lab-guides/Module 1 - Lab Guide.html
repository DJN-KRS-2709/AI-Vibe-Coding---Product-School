Module 1 · Hands-On Lab Guide

Activate Vibe Coding Speed

01
Lab 1 · Hands-On Exercise · 15 minutes

Your First Vibe Build in Lovable

TASK: Dive straight into the vibe coding cycle by building a functional, shareable prototype from scratch. Move from a vague problem to a clickable solution in under 15 minutes. Success = a shareable link, not a polished product.

1

Choose Your Scenario

Before opening Lovable, pick one of the four scenarios below to guide your build.

🎯
Heads up: In Module 2 you'll commit to one of these scenarios and carry it through the rest of the course for your final project. What you build today is a head start, so pick something you'd want to keep building on. Today is exploration — you're not locked in yet. If you can't decide, pick Scenario 1 or 4 — they work for almost any PM role.
Scenario 1
The Retention Engine
B2B SaaS losing 30% of customers in 90 days. Users "sign up, poke around, and ghost." Team invites matter. What's the real blocker?
Scenario 2
The Internal Tool Nobody Uses
CRM with 18% adoption. Reps say "too many clicks." CEO wants Gong. Can you fix adoption without replacing the tool?
Scenario 3
The Marketplace Trust Problem
Bookings flat. Zero-review providers can't get their first customer. How do you solve cold-start trust?
Scenario 4
The Dashboard Nobody Reads
Analytics with 60% bounce rate. Power users love it, new users flee. How do you serve both?
2

Launch the Build

Open Lovable and start your project. You have two paths to get moving immediately. Read both options and then choose the one you wish to explore.

Option A · Copy & Customize (The Starter Template)

Use one of the baseline prompts and replace the brackets with your specific details to jumpstart the AI.

Best for Scenario 3
Prompt Option 1 "Build me a [community/social] feature for [fitness/sales/customer] users. Include a [matching screen / leaderboard / shared feed]."
Best for Scenarios 1 & 4
Prompt Option 2 "Build me a dashboard that shows [account health / sales pipeline / user activity]. Include a [filter / list / chart]."
Best for Scenario 2
Prompt Option 3 "Build me a [type of app] that helps users [one action]. Include [one UI element]."

Option B · The First Screen Method (Pure Instinct)

Visualize the very first thing your user sees when they log in. Describe that specific UI to Lovable and hit enter.

  • Focus on speed and describe the layout, the main buttons, and the primary data points.
  • Don't over-engineer. Let the AI handle the initial "vibe" while you focus on getting a functional interface running.
  • Ask yourself: "What's the first screen your user would see? What's on it?" Then write your prompt, or fill in the template:
First-Screen Template "Build me a [that screen] for [that user] with [those key elements]."
💡
Credit-Saver Tip: If you want to plan a complex or highly detailed prompt, refine your text in a free LLM like ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude first. Once the logic is tight, paste the final version into Lovable to save your tokens for the actual build.
3

Iterate and Refine

If the first output isn't right, do not start over from scratch. Use the chat bar in Lovable to steer the AI toward the right solution by enabling "Plan" before you prompt.

  1. Use follow-up instructions in the chat (e.g., "move the search bar to the top right" or "make this button open a modal") to fix specific issues immediately.
  • If you are stuck on a specific design detail for more than 2 minutes, move on. Your goal is a functional prototype, not a completed design system.
  1. You must have a live, shareable link before the 15 minutes are up. Stop iterating with 2 minutes left to ensure your project URL is ready for the peer review swap.
4

Generate Your Shareable Link

As the 15-minute timer nears the end, you must ensure your prototype is ready for eyes other than your own.

⚠️
Critical Note: You will be randomly assigned a partner for a Show and Swap peer review immediately following this exercise. You cannot review a blank screen, so ensure you have a functional link ready when the timer hits zero.
  1. Click through your main navigation and buttons to ensure the flow makes sense. If you have time to fix any final catches, prompt quick!
  2. Take a screenshot of your finished product and store it in a safe place. You'll need it for a before-and-after post in Slack later today.
  3. In the top right corner of your Lovable screen, click the "Share" button and select "Share preview" to generate a link for your peer review swap. Copy and keep the link ready.
Peer Review · 10 minutes · Breakout

Show & Swap Your Prototype

Spend 3 minutes watching your partner explore your prototype in total silence. Do not give a tour or say "click that." If they get stuck, let them stay stuck — that is your data! After 3 minutes, ask these questions and take notes.

Q1 What you understood immediately about the product's purpose. ✍️ Write your partner's answers here.
Q2 What confused you about the prototype. ✍️ Write your partner's answers here.
Q3 What assumption you thought this prototype was testing. ✍️ Write your partner's answers here.
02
Lab 2 · Hands-On Exercise · 15 minutes

Strategically Refine Your Build in Lovable

TASK: Return to your initial prototype from Lab 1 and apply strategic upgrades to move it from a vibe sketch to a professional tool. Prove you can steer the AI toward excellence by refining the visual design and adding genuine functional interactivity. Success = a final prototype that feels like something you would proudly show to a VP.

1

Identify the Current Gap

Open your Lab 1 prototype in Lovable. Spend 60 seconds identifying the weakest section or the most critical gap in your V1.

  • Use the feedback you received from your partner during the Show and Swap session. Where did they get stuck, or what part of your explanation required a workaround because the UI didn't show it?
  • Pick your battle: Is it the visual polish, or does your prototype lack the functionality to tell a real story?
2

Upgrade and Iterate with Intent

Select two of the following paths to transform your rough sketch. Do not try to do all four; focus on depth in two areas. If you cannot fix a specific bug within 3 minutes, pivot to a different upgrade. It is better to have a polished design than a broken filter.

💡
Note: You can complete the templates below with your own details to move quickly, or work with an LLM to architect your own custom prompts if you have a specific vision in mind.
⚠️
Watch the Clock: Stop iterating with 2 minutes left. You must have a live, shareable link ready for the second peer review swap.
Path A · Visual Level-Up
Design Match

Use a screenshot from Mobbin to clone professional colors, layouts, and navigation.

Prompt "I am uploading a screenshot of [Company]. Update my dashboard's color palette, border radius, and typography to match this aesthetic exactly."
Path B · Functional Level-Up
Add Interactivity

Configure a functional filter, a working detail view, or a form that validates data — prove the app can handle real user behavior.

Prompt "Make the [List or Gallery name, e.g., Workout List] filterable by [Category, e.g., Difficulty Level]. When a user clicks a specific [UI Element, e.g., Card], open a [Panel or Modal] showing the [Specific Data, e.g., exercise video and instructions] for that selection."
Path C · Precision Level-Up
Surgical Refinement

Use a focused prompt to fix one specific section without changing the rest of the page.

Prompt "Focus only on the [section name, e.g., sidebar]. Change the [element] to a [new element, e.g., dropdown menu], and move the [component] to the [new location]."
Path D · Brand Alignment
Existing Product Track

Match your actual company's design system by extracting styles from your live site or Figma. Layer your new feature on top of the existing brand.

  • How to get the data from Figma: Right-click a component or frame, select "Copy/Paste as" > "Copy as CSS." Paste this code into the Lovable interface.
Prompt "Update the UI components to match [Company Name]'s branding. Use [Hex Code] for primary buttons and ensure the [specific feature, e.g., navigation element] matches our product's current structure."
3

Capture Your Transformation

Before the timer hits zero, you must have your updated version ready to show and documented for the final showcase.

  1. Take a screenshot of your upgraded prototype. You will need this to post your before-and-after transformation on Slack later.
  2. Since you are upgrading the same prototype, your shareable URL will likely remain the same. Refresh your screen and test before you recopy the link to ensure the latest changes are live.
  3. Copy your link and have it ready. You are about to be moved into a new breakout room for a second peer review where you must demonstrate your transformation.
Peer Review · 10 minutes · Breakout

Swap & Review Your Refined Prototype

Spend 3 minutes watching your partner explore your prototype in total silence. Do not give a tour or say "click that." If they get stuck, let them stay stuck — that is your data! After 3 minutes, ask these questions and take notes.

Q1 Does this feel like a "real" product or just a visual mockup? ✍️ Write your partner's answers here.
Q2 Where did the interactivity fail to meet your expectations as a user? ✍️ Write your partner's answers here.
Q3 Would you show this to your VP as a credible representation of a solution? ✍️ Write your partner's answers here.
