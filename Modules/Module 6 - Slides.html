What's Actually Happening on Your Product?

Two ways to see what's happening on your deployed product:

  • Lovable Insights — Built-in analytics dashboard. Page views, user sessions, most-used features.
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — Industry-standard tracking. Traffic sources, user flows, bounce rates, conversion funnels.

The data is already being collected. You just need to look at it.

What to Measure

Engagement
  • How many users signed up?
  • How many completed the core flow?
  • Which features used most? Ignored?
Retention
  • Do users come back?
  • Where do users drop off?
Quality
  • Page load time — under 3s?
  • Error rates — dead ends?
  • Mobile vs. desktop usage
Impact
  • Does behavior match your M2 hypothesis?
  • Are users doing what you predicted?

The metric that matters most: does user behavior confirm or contradict your original problem statement?

Teaching

The PM's New Superpower

AI + Data + Problem Statement

Traditional: export → spreadsheet → charts → doc → days.
AI-powered: metrics + problem statement → AI → evaluation in minutes.

The formula:

  • Pull your product metrics (from Lovable insights or GA4)
  • Pull your problem statement from your Living PRD (M4)
  • Pull your M2 hypothesis
  • Feed all three to ChatGPT or Gemini

The AI doesn't replace your judgment. It accelerates your analysis.

Teaching

Evidence-Based Improvement
vs. Guess-Based Improvement

Most PMs iterate by instinct. Evidence-based iteration uses data to decide what to fix.

The Iteration Prompt Pattern:

"Based on this analysis: [paste AI findings].
The highest-impact improvement is: [specific finding].
Update the product to address this: [describe the change].
Keep everything else working."

Spotify calls this "tweak it" — the fifth stage: understand it, think it, build it, ship it, tweak it.
