The Iteration Mindset

Shipping a prototype isn't the finish line — it's the start of the feedback loop.

Your goal: replace "I think" with "the data shows." From this point forward, your intuition is just a hypothesis the data either proves or kills.

1
Feature Prioritisation
Find the 20% that drives 80% of value
Every V1 has ghost features — things you thought were critical that no one touches. Data identifies the 20% of your product driving 80% of the value so you stop wasting engineering cycles on the other 80%.
2
Friction Identification
See the leaks you can't see
You know how the app works because you built it. Real users don't. Data exposes the leaks and dead ends in your UI that your own bias makes you blind to.
3
Hypothesis Validation
Audit the workflow you designed
Compare the workflow you designed against how humans actually navigate it. Data is the only objective way to see if your solution is operating the way you engineered it to.
The reframe Every decision from here is backed by evidence, not instinct. Your intuition gets you to a hypothesis; the data either funds it or kills it.
Metrics That Matter

3–5 signals beat 50 — every time.

Analysis paralysis is the biggest killer of V2. Narrow the lens to a handful of signals that map directly to your problem statement.

🚦
Engagement
The core flow. How many users signed up? Which features are used most — and which are ignored ghosts?
🔁
Retention
Your honesty metric. Do users return after their first session? Where exactly do they drop off in the flow?
⚙️
Quality
The "is it broken" check. Is the page load under 3 seconds? Are users hitting dead ends or high error rates?
🎯
Impact
The hypothesis check. Does user behaviour match your M2 hypothesis? Is what you predicted actually happening?
North Star Does user behaviour confirm or contradict your original problem statement? If the data says no, your V2 isn't an update — it's a pivot.
Three Layers of Analytics

What users do · what they feel · what they say.

Shipping is 50% of the job. The other 50% is architecting how you'll see friction before the user realises it's there.

1
Internal · Passive System
Surface-level engagement
Tool: Lovable Insights (built-in).
Data: active users, sessions, page views, click depth.

Day-zero pulse. No setup, no extra code — it's been collecting since you deployed in M5. This is Lab 1.
2
External · Passive Behavioural
Deep-funnel signal
Tool: Google Analytics 4 (optional post-class).
Data: traffic sources, bounce rates, conversion paths.

Moves beyond clicks to the actual user journey — where leaks happen and where users abandon the flow before completing a goal.
3
Custom · Active Sentiment
Direct qualitative data
Tool: in-product capture wired to Supabase.
Data: star ratings, bug reports, feature requests.

Custom feedback loops you own — like a "Report Issue" button piped into your own tables. Data you own is data you can act on.

AI-Driven Product Iteration.
Stop crunching CSVs. Feed your signal to an LLM. Get a prioritised backlog in minutes. Translate one finding into one surgical prompt.
AI-Powered Analysis · The PM's New Superpower

From data cruncher to decision maker.

You've been here before. Days cleaning data, building charts, writing a 10-page findings doc no one reads. In the AI era, you move from analysis to decision in minutes — but you still own the call.

🕰️ Old Way · Manual Analysis
Days of exports + pivot tables
  • Export CSVs from every tool.
  • Build pivot tables in spreadsheets.
  • Spend 6 hours writing a "findings" doc.
  • Hope someone reads it before the product moves on.
⚡ New Way · AI-Powered Synthesis
Minutes to a prioritised backlog
  • Feed problem statement + hypothesis + metrics + peer feedback to the LLM.
  • Ask: "is this solving the problem? What are the top 3 fixes, ranked by impact?"
  • Get a synthesised, ranked backlog in seconds.
  • You own the final decision — the AI does not.
The Strategic Prompt · paste into your LLM of choice

I built a product to solve this problem: [problem statement]. My hypothesis was: [hypothesis]. Here are the metrics from my live product: [paste Lovable Insights data]. Here is the peer feedback I received: [paste Slack feedback].

Evaluate: is this product solving the problem? What does the data suggest? What are the top 3 things I should improve, ranked by likely impact?

The discipline The AI provides the synthesis — you provide the conviction. Pick one finding. Not three.
From Data Signal to Technical Fix

Every prompt now starts with "based on the data..."

The AI tells you what is wrong — it's a list of problems. It doesn't tell you how to fix it. This is where you move from analysis to architecture.

🧠
From "Verbose" to "Persona"
Persona Refinement
If users are overwhelmed by text, don't ask for "less copy" — that's a tweak. Hardcode a persona into the system: "act as an efficiency expert" — and the entire app's voice shifts permanently.

"Based on the data showing 70% bounce, refactor all copy to the voice of an efficiency expert — short, declarative, action-first."
🔀
From "Bounce" to "Branching"
Visual Branching
If GA4 shows a 70% bounce on a screen, the AI might suggest "the UI is too complex." Translate that into a different mental model — swap a list for a board, or a wizard for a single page.

"Based on the 70% bounce on /onboarding, swap the multi-step wizard for a single Kanban board the user can move cards on."
🛡️
From "Functional Gap" to "Guardrail"
Hard IF/THEN Rules
When Insights show submissions failing in a specific shape, the AI flags a logic error. Translate that into a guardrail — an unbreakable IF/THEN rule the system can never violate.

"Based on the data showing duplicate invites, IF the recipient_email already exists in invites for this sender, THEN block the submit and show an inline warning."
The pattern Every M6 prompt starts with "based on the data showing..." — that's how you stop feature-creeping and start surgically repairing.

Section 04 · Hands-On Lab
Run an AI-Driven Iteration Sprint.
One finding, one prompt, one redeploy. Push the brief to GitHub.
Individual Lab · 15 minutes

Close the loop: data → AI → one fix → redeploy.

Pull together everything you gathered, run the strategic prompt, pick the single highest-impact finding, ship the fix, push to GitHub. Resist the temptation to fix everything — one finding, one change.

1
📥
Gather your inputs
Problem statement + hypothesis from your Living PRD, Lovable Insights metrics from Lab 1, and the peer feedback from your Slack thread.
2
🤖
Run the strategic prompt
Paste the prompt from slide 16 into ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini. Replace the four placeholders. Read the ranked backlog.
3
🎯
Pick one finding
The single highest-impact recommendation. Not three. Not the safest. The one that moves the needle.
4
⚡
Write your "based on the data" prompt
Pattern: "Based on the user data showing [finding], update [specific part] to [specific change]. Keep all existing functionality working." Pick Persona, Branching, or Guardrail.
5
🧪
Test, audit, then redeploy
Does the change work as expected? Did anything else break? If yes, re-prompt before redeploying. Once clean, click Publish.
6
📤
Push to GitHub
Commit Iteration-Sprint-Brief.md to 06-iteration/ with the AI analysis, the chosen finding, the implementation prompt, and the redeployed URL.
