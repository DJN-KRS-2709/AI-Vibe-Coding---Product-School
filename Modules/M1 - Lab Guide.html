Hit blank-page syndrome? Two escape routes below.

Escape Route 1: Copy and Customize

Starter Prompts — Replace the [brackets] and go
"Build me a [community/social] feature for [fitness/sales/customer] users. Include a [matching screen / leaderboard / shared feed]."

"Build me a dashboard that shows [account health / sales pipeline / user activity]. Include a [filter / list / chart]."

"Build me a [type of app] that helps users [one action]. Include [one UI element]."

Escape Route 2: First Screen Method

Ask yourself → Then prompt
What's the first screen your user would see? What's on it?

→ "Build me a [that screen] for [that user] with [those key elements]."

Example: "Build me a sales rep dashboard with recent calls, CRM sync button, and quick-note field."

Show & Swap

Breakout Pairs · 10 minutes

You are NOT allowed to explain your prototype.
Share the link. Watch them use it. Say nothing.

1
Person A shares their link
3 minutes of silence
Person B clicks through it as a real user. No guidance. No "click there." Just watch where they go, what confuses them, what they ignore.
2
Person B gives feedback
2 minutes
Three things:
1
What I understood immediately
2
What confused me
3
What assumption does this seem to test?
3
Switch roles. Repeat.
Person B shares their link. Person A becomes the user.
💡
Why silence matters: Your users won't have you standing next to them explaining things. You'll learn more from watching 3 minutes of confusion than from any feedback form.

Level Up Your Build

Hands-On Lab 2 · 15 minutes

Take your Lab 1 prototype. Don't start over. Choose at least two upgrades:

🎨
Design Match
Go to Mobbin (mobbin.com). Find a real product with a similar layout. Screenshot it. Use it as context in your next prompt. "Match this look and feel."
⚡
Add Interactivity
Make something work. A filter that filters. A detail view that expands. A form that validates. Static → clickable → testable.
🎯
Refine Without Rebuilding
Find the weakest section. Fix only that part with a focused prompt. Tell the AI what to change AND what NOT to change.
🏢
Existing Product Track
Screenshot your actual company product. Match its look and feel. Add your new feature on top. "Wait, is this already in our product?"
🔑
The key skill: You're steering, not starting over. Each prompt gets you closer. The instructor just showed you how to go from generic AI output to stakeholder-ready in 6 prompts. Apply the same pattern: design → interactivity → refine.

Did It Get Better?

New Breakout Pairs · 10 minutes

New partner — different person from Round 1. Share both links: your original Lab 1 build AND your upgraded Lab 2 build.

1
Partner reviews both versions
5 minutes per person
They explore the original first, then the upgrade. The comparison is the point — same product, different quality level.
2
Three feedback questions
1
What improved most noticeably?
2
Does this feel like a real product now?
3
Would you show this to your VP?

If the answer to "Would you show this to your VP?" changed from no to yes — you just crossed the line from toy to tool.
