Speaker Notes

Setup (1 min): "Open the Structured Prompt Builder. Select your scenario at the top — it'll tell you which block matters most for your specific problem. Then fill in each block using the three artifacts you just prepared. Block 1 gets your context data. Block 2 gets your screenshots. Block 3 gets your validation brief as guardrails. Block 4 defines what Lovable should actually build."

On the workflow: "Don't freestyle. The Prompt Builder exists because we've seen what happens when people just type into Lovable cold — you get generic output. The whole point of Part 1 was preparing these inputs. Now use them. Paste, don't retype."

The guardrail warning: "Block 3 has a red warning for a reason. The number one mistake in Module 1 was people building impressive-looking strategy dashboards instead of interactive products. Your prototype should be something a real user clicks through to test your hypothesis. If nobody can interact with it, it's a slide deck."

Credits strategy: "Review your assembled prompt before pasting it into Lovable. Read it out loud. Does it sound like clear instructions to a developer? If not, tighten it. You can even run it through ChatGPT first — zero credits. When it reads right, paste it into Lovable in one shot."

Release: "Go. 25 minutes. When your prototype is live, grab the shareable link and test it in an incognito window. If the link works, you're ready for the peer review. Post the link in Slack."
