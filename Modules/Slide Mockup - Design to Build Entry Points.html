Speaker Notes

Text-to-App: "This is your 'blank canvas' option. If you have a problem statement but no visual reference, just describe what you need using the prompt anatomy from slide 14 — Context, Reference, Constraint, Output. It's great for speed, but you'll get more generic results without a visual anchor."

Screenshot-to-App: "This is the highest-ROI move for most of you. Go to Mobbin — it's free — find a real product screen that's close to what you want (a pricing page, a dashboard, an onboarding flow). Screenshot it, paste it into Lovable, and say 'match this look and feel, then add my feature.' Or screenshot your own product and build on top of it. The AI matches the layout, fonts, and spacing automatically."

Design-to-Code: "If your company has a Figma design system, you can import it directly. Lovable's MCP integration connects to Figma — we'll go much deeper on this in Module 3. For today, just know it exists. Most of you will use Screenshot-to-App in the lab."

Quick tip: "The fastest path to a coherent prototype is feeding in what already exists. Don't spend 5 prompts describing a dashboard layout when you can screenshot one from Mobbin in 10 seconds."
